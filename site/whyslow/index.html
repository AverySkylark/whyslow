How it works

  1. Open the page you want to check and click the WhySlow icon.
  2. Choose "Reload & analyze" for a realistic first visit, or "Analyze tab" for what is already loaded. You can also type any address under "Test URL".
  3. Read the snapshot, then copy the report and paste it into Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or any other assistant. The report already contains instructions, so there is nothing to write.

What you get

  • A quick snapshot. Server response, first paint, main content, layout shift, requests, total size, third party share, render blocking files, script blocking time, domains and page size, each rated good, needs improvement or poor.
  • Likely causes, ranked. Each one comes with evidence and file names.
  • An AI ready report. It holds a built in prompt, test context, ranked findings, where time and bytes go, the slowest and largest files, and a plain "limits of this data" section so the AI does not guess.
  • Fix and retest. Every run is saved on your device. After you change something, I show what improved, what got worse and which files appeared or disappeared. Add a note such as "enabled page cache" and it goes into the report.
  • Exports. A Markdown report, copy to clipboard, and a CSV of every request.
  • Light and dark. It follows your system, or you can switch in the popup.

Private by design

WhySlow has no servers, no account, no analytics and no ads. All measuring and all saved history stay in your browser. The only things that leave are the reports you choose to copy or save.

Good to know

  • Timings come from your own browser and connection. Repeat the test and compare rather than treating one run as exact.
  • Causes on the server, such as the database, CMS or PHP, can only be inferred from the server response time. The report says so.
  • Chrome blocks extensions on chrome:// pages and the Chrome Web Store.
